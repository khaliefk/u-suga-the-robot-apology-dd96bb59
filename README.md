A two-line robot confession for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59): one sentence owning a specific mistake, one sentence naming the lesson and the fix that follows from it.

## Entry

> I am sorry I washed your passport with your navy jeans because I emptied your pockets without looking.
>
> From now on I will shake every pocket out over the table and check for cards and paper before I start the wash.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
